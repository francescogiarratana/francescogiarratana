## 👋 About me

I'm an MSc student in **Computer Engineering at the University of Trieste (UniTS)**, working at the intersection of **cybersecurity, backend engineering and secure AI**. I like systems that are defensible by design: clear trust boundaries, auditable behaviour, and compliance built in from day one rather than bolted on.

- 🛡️ **Compliance-by-design** — defensible architectures, business continuity and controls aligned with **NIS-2** and Zero Trust principles.
- 🤖 **Secure & agentic AI** — privacy-first, on-premise AI architectures with verifiable audit trails, mitigating data exfiltration and prompt-injection risks.
- ⚙️ **Distributed backend** — scalable services in **Java / Spring Boot** and **Python**, containerised and deployed over private mesh networks.
